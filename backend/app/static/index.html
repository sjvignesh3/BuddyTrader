📌 Fundamental Pointers — Quick Reference

🚨 Position Sizing Rules

  • Large Cap → Max 5% of portfolio per stock
  • Mid Cap → Max 3% of portfolio per stock
  • Small Cap → Max 1.5% of portfolio per stock

📉 Buy Only When It Falls

  • Large Cap falls 20–30% from peak
  • Mid Cap falls 35–50% from peak
  • Small Cap falls 50–70% from peak
  • Never buy above 200 DMA

✅ Must-Have Conditions

  • PE < 70 & PE < 5 Yr Median PE
  • Net Debt/Equity < 0.25
  • ROCE > 15 (ROE > 15 for banks)
  • TTM Net Profit > ₹250 Cr
  • Pledged < 5% • Public Holding < 30%
  • Sales & Profit near All-Time High (≥90%)
  • OPM stable or increasing

⚠️ Watch Out

  • Interest reducing profit → excessive borrowing
  • Exceptional income → abnormal items
  • Tax payment anomalies → manipulation risk
  • Market Cap classification: Large (1-100), Mid (101-250), Small (251+)
